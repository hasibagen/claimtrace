# caffeine-dose-response-pfc-hbo-is-inverted-u

## 主张陈述

Under partial sleep restriction, the prefrontal hemodynamic response to caffeine is non-monotonic: it peaks near 200 mg and declines at 400 mg.

## 推理桥

Omnibus dose effect with pre-planned contrasts (200 > 100, 200 > 400) plus a behavioral trend in the same ranking and rising side effects at 400 mg.

- 推理类型: `empirical_inference`
